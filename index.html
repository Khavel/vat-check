Privacy Policy — VAT Check

Last updated: November 2026

VAT Check is a Google Sheets add-on that validates EU VAT numbers against the official VIES register of the European Commission.

What data is processed

The add-on reads the VAT numbers you select in your spreadsheet and sends each one to the European Commission's VIES service to check whether it is valid. The VIES service returns the registered business name and address for valid numbers.

Where data is stored

Everything happens inside your own Google Sheet:

  • Results are written to a VAT Results sheet you control.
  • A hidden _VAT_CACHE sheet stores the results of previous lookups with a timestamp, so repeat lookups are instant and you have an audit trail.

We operate no server. We do not store, transmit, or receive your data anywhere except the two destinations above: your own Google Sheet and the official VIES service.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no cookies.
  • No data is sold or shared with any third party.
  • No data leaves your Google account except the VAT number sent to VIES (an EU institution) for validation.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact the developer at the support email listed on the Google Workspace Marketplace listing.
