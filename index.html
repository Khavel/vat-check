Validate EU VAT numbers in Google Sheets™ with the official VIES register — in bulk, with a timestamped audit trail.

What it does
  • Validates any EU VAT number (ES, DE, FR, IT, NL, …) using the European Commission's official VIES service.
  • Returns the registered business name and address.
  • Writes results to a dedicated VAT Results sheet — never over your data.
  • Caches every lookup for 30 days, with a hidden audit-trail sheet.
Pricing

Free: 25 checks per month. Cached lookups are always free.
