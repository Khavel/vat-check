Support — VAT Check

Need help? Email khavel112@gmail.com.

To get a useful answer fast, include:

  • the VAT number you checked (or a made-up example with the same format),
  • the country,
  • what you expected and what you saw,
  • roughly when you ran it.

Known behaviour worth knowing first:

  • "INVALID" means VIES does not have that number registered — often a typo, or the business is not VAT-registered.
  • "SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE" means the relevant EU member state's register was temporarily offline. Retry later.
  • Results are cached for 30 days; a number you already checked won't be re-fetched.
